Section-by-Section Blueprint

1
Hero Section Hormozi: Lead with Outcome
The transformation promise — not the bio
›
Headline — speak to the pain + desired outcome. "Finally feel like yourself again" beats "Compassionate therapy for your journey."
Subheadline — who she helps + how long it takes. Specificity builds credibility. "Most clients feel a real shift within 6 sessions."
Primary CTA — "Book a Free 15-Min Call." Low commitment, high value. Hormozi: make the first step near-zero friction.
Trust bar — 4 data points: client count, rating, modalities, insurance. This sits under the CTA to pre-handle objections immediately.
No photo yet — Hormozi: don't front-load credentials. A photo of Jennifer should come after the outcome promise is established.
2
Problem Agitation Block Hormozi: Identify the Pain
Make the visitor feel seen before offering a solution
›
Header — "Sound familiar?" or "Does any of this feel like you?"
3–4 bullet pain points — written in the client's words, not clinical language. E.g., "You wake up already exhausted before the day starts." "You snap at people you love and don't know why."
Hormozi rule — name the external problem, internal emotion, and philosophical frustration. That's what makes copy resonate deeply.
Transition line — "It doesn't have to stay this way." Bridges pain to solution without being dismissive.
3
The Method / How It Works Hormozi: The Vehicle
Show the path to the transformation
›
3-step visual process — Step 1: Free Discovery Call → Step 2: Personalized Plan → Step 3: Real Progress. Simple, not clinical.
Unique Mechanism — name Jennifer's approach. E.g., "The Clarity Method" — a brief proprietary-sounding name for her therapy approach makes her stand out from generic therapists.
Hormozi: the vehicle must feel new — even if it's standard CBT, how Jennifer frames it should feel tailored and specific to the niche she serves.
4
Social Proof / Testimonials Hormozi: Proof Stack
Earned credibility beats stated credibility
›
Lead with outcomes — testimonials should describe the before/after, not just "Jennifer is great." "I went from panic attacks 3x a week to sleeping through the night." is gold.
Minimum 4–6 testimonials — with first name + therapy type (e.g., "Anxiety, OC") for specificity. Real = credible.
Video testimonial — even one 30-second video clip outperforms 10 text quotes. Prioritize getting at least one.
Star rating display — pull from Psychology Today, Google, or Yelp. Show the platform logo + count.
5
Objection Handling / FAQ Hormozi: Pre-handle Doubt
Answer "but what about…" before they leave
›
Insurance? — "Yes, I accept [X, Y, Z] plans. For others, I provide superbills for reimbursement."
In-person or telehealth? — "Both. You choose what works best for you each session."
How long will therapy take? — "Most clients see meaningful change in 8–12 sessions. We'll set a clear plan on our first call."
Hormozi rule — every FAQ answer should end with a micro-CTA or a confidence-builder. Don't just answer — close.
6
Final CTA Section Hormozi: The Close
One last reason to act now
›
Restate the transformation — "You deserve to feel good in your own mind. That starts with one 15-minute call."
Scarcity / urgency — "Jennifer currently has [X] openings this month." — real scarcity, never fake.
Risk reversal — "The discovery call is completely free. No obligation, no pressure." Eliminate the last reason not to click.
Single button — "Book My Free Call →" Only one action. No distractions.

04 — AI Module

AI Chatbot Spec

A floating chat widget powered by an AI layer (GPT-4 or Claude). Handles FAQs, qualifies leads, and books appointments — 24/7 without Jennifer lifting a finger.

Chatbot Flow

1
Greeting + Soft Qualifier

"Hi, I'm here to help you connect with Jennifer. Are you looking for support with anxiety, relationships, or something else?"

2
FAQ Handling

Answers common questions about insurance, session format, rates, and availability from a knowledge base.

3
Warm Handoff to Booking

"It sounds like Jennifer would be a great fit. Want me to show you her next available slots?"

4
Calendar Embed or Link

Pushes visitor directly to Calendly / SimplePractice booking within the chat or opens a modal.

5
Lead Capture Fallback

If no booking happens, collects name + email + concern. Sends to Jennifer's CRM or email via Zapier.

Tone — warm, unhurried, empathetic. No robotic phrases.
05 — Booking System

Appointment Booking

📅

Recommended: SimplePractice

Purpose-built for therapists. Handles intake forms, billing, telehealth, HIPAA compliance, and calendar sync. Best all-in-one.

Recommended
🔗

Alternative: Calendly + Typeform

Calendly for scheduling, Typeform for intake. Cheaper to start, but two tools to manage. Good for MVP phase.

Budget Option
⚡

Zapier Automation

On new booking: send confirmation email, add to CRM, notify Jennifer via Slack or SMS. Zero manual work.

Automation Layer

Booking Flow

Step 1 — Visitor clicks CTA → Taken to /book page with embedded calendar widget.
Step 2 — Selects date + time → Fills short intake form (name, concern, insurance).
Step 3 — Confirmation email sent automatically with Zoom/address link and intake paperwork.
Step 4 (Automation) — Zapier notifies Jennifer, adds client to her CRM, and sends a reminder 24h before the session.
